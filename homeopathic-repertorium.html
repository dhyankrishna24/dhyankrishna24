Mind & Emotional Symptoms

Aconitum Napellus

Aconitum napellus (Monkshood)

Key Indication: Sudden acute conditions, panic attacks, fear

Mental Symptoms:

  • Great fear and anxiety, fear of death
  • Restlessness and agitation
  • Panic attacks with palpitations
  • Anxiety after shock or fright

Ignatia Amara

Ignatia amara (St. Ignatius Bean)

Key Indication: Grief, emotional upset, hysteria

Mental Symptoms:

  • Acute grief and disappointment
  • Mood swings, sighing
  • Suppressed emotions
  • Lump in throat sensation
  • Effects of loss or bereavement

Arsenicum Album

Arsenicum album (White Arsenic)

Key Indication: Anxiety, restlessness, perfectionism

Mental Symptoms:

  • Anxiety about health and security
  • Restlessness, especially at night
  • Fastidious and perfectionist
  • Fear of being alone

Head & Headache

Belladonna

Atropa belladonna (Deadly Nightshade)

Key Indication: Sudden, violent onset; throbbing headaches

Head Symptoms:

  • Throbbing, pulsating headache
  • Hot, red face with cold extremities
  • Sudden onset of symptoms
  • Worse from light, noise, jarring
  • Better from pressure and sitting up

Gelsemium

Gelsemium sempervirens (Yellow Jasmine)

Key Indication: Dull headache, heaviness, drowsiness

Head Symptoms:

  • Dull, heavy headache at back of head
  • Droopy eyelids, drowsiness
  • Headache better after urination
  • Anticipatory anxiety

Natrum Muriaticum

Natrum muriaticum (Sodium Chloride)

Key Indication: Hammering headaches, sun headache

Head Symptoms:

  • Bursting, hammering headache
  • Headache from sunrise to sunset
  • Worse from sun exposure
  • Associated with eye strain

Respiratory System

Bryonia Alba

Bryonia alba (White Bryony)

Key Indication: Dry cough, chest pain, worse from motion

Respiratory Symptoms:

  • Dry, painful cough
  • Chest pain worse from motion
  • Must hold chest when coughing
  • Great thirst for large quantities
  • Worse from any movement

Phosphorus

Phosphorus

Key Indication: Chest complaints, hoarseness, bronchitis

Respiratory Symptoms:

  • Hoarseness, losing voice
  • Hard, dry, tight cough
  • Chest tightness and oppression
  • Better from cold drinks (but vomited when warm)
  • Bleeding tendency

Antimonium Tartaricum

Antimonium tartaricum (Tartar Emetic)

Key Indication: Rattling cough, difficulty expectorating

Respiratory Symptoms:

  • Loose, rattling cough
  • Difficulty raising mucus
  • Drowsiness with respiratory symptoms
  • Better from expectoration

Digestive System

Nux Vomica

Strychnos nux-vomica (Poison Nut)

Key Indication: Digestive complaints from overindulgence

Digestive Symptoms:

  • Nausea and vomiting after eating
  • Constipation with ineffectual urging
  • Heartburn and indigestion
  • Worse from coffee, alcohol, spices
  • Irritable and oversensitive

Carbo Vegetabilis

Carbo vegetabilis (Vegetable Charcoal)

Key Indication: Gas, bloating, flatulence

Digestive Symptoms:

  • Excessive flatulence and bloating
  • Slow digestion
  • Desire for fresh air
  • Better from belching
  • Weakness and exhaustion

Pulsatilla

Pulsatilla nigricans (Wind Flower)

Key Indication: Digestive upset from fatty foods

Digestive Symptoms:

  • Aversion to fat and rich food
  • Changeable symptoms
  • Desire for fresh, open air
  • Thirstless with complaints
  • Gentle, weepy disposition

Skin Conditions

Sulphur

Sulphur

Key Indication: Itchy skin conditions, burning sensations

Skin Symptoms:

  • Intense itching, worse from warmth
  • Burning sensations
  • Dirty, unhealthy looking skin
  • Worse from bathing
  • Hot feet, throws covers off at night

Graphites

Graphites

Key Indication: Thick, honey-like discharge, skin cracks

Skin Symptoms:

  • Eruptions with thick, sticky discharge
  • Cracks and fissures in skin
  • Worse behind ears, in folds
  • Keloid formations
  • Chilly patient

Rhus Toxicodendron

Rhus toxicodendron (Poison Ivy)

Key Indication: Itchy blisters, worse at rest

Skin Symptoms:

  • Intense itching with red, swollen skin
  • Small blisters or vesicles
  • Better from warmth and motion
  • Worse from cold and rest
  • Restlessness

Fever & Infections

Ferrum Phosphoricum

Ferrum phosphoricum (Iron Phosphate)

Key Indication: First stage of inflammation, low fever

Fever Symptoms:

  • Low to moderate fever
  • First stage of any inflammation
  • Face alternately pale and flushed
  • No clear picture of symptoms
  • Better from cold applications

Pyrogenium

Pyrogenium

Key Indication: Septic conditions, offensive discharges

Fever Symptoms:

  • High fever with relatively slow pulse
  • All discharges are offensive
  • Restlessness despite exhaustion
  • Bed feels too hard
  • Septic states

Oscillococcinum

Anas barbariae hepatis et cordis extractum

Key Indication: Flu-like symptoms

Fever Symptoms:

  • Fever with chills
  • Body aches
  • Fatigue
  • Best when taken at first sign of flu

  • Morning: Nux Vomica (digestive complaints)
  • Evening: Pulsatilla (emotional symptoms)

Temperature Modalities:

  • Better from Heat: Arsenicum, Rhus Tox, Nux Vomica

  • Worse from Heat: Pulsatilla, Apis
